Read the full transcript

Yard Card Jobs

This is Yard Card Jobs, a simple app for running a yard sign business, from the first booking to the final invoice.

It runs on your phone, and keeps everything in one place: your jobs, your crew, and your customer emails.

As you watch, think about what you like, what's confusing, and what you'd add.

Your business

Let's start with the one-time setup. Tap Business at the bottom of the screen.

Type your business name, your email and phone number, and how customers pay you, like Venmo, Zelle or cash.

The sign-off is the friendly line at the end of every email.

Logo and app icon

Next, your logo. You can upload one, or use a colored badge with your initials.

Watch the badge change as you pick a color. It also becomes the app's icon on your phone.

Review links

Then paste your review links for Facebook, Instagram and Google.

They're added to the thank-you email, so happy customers can leave a review in one tap.

Local teams

Under Teams, type the area you serve, and the local high schools, youth leagues and clubs, one per line.

Michigan pro and college teams are already built in. Your local teams show first when you pick a team for a booking.

Sign inventory

Inventory is where you count the signs you own, like two birthday banners or twelve confetti stakes. Number signs, from zero to ten, have their own rows.

The app then shows how many of each sign are free on a job's dates, and warns you if you're short.

Tap Save business details. You only need to do this once.

Your crew and your signs

Next is Crew. List your workers with their phone numbers, and choose how early the app reminds you to text them.

The crew text is a template. The words in curly brackets fill in by themselves for each job, like the address and the signs to bring.

Below that is your sign checklist for each occasion, and example photos of past jobs that show the crew what to build.

Everything on one screen

Now the Jobs screen. It shows signs that are up in yards, set-ups coming soon, and finished jobs.

The colored alerts at the top tell you what's due, like texting your crew before tomorrow's set-up, or reminding a customer about pick-up.

Add a booking

To add a booking, tap New booking.

Fill in the customer's name, email, phone and address.

Then the sign: the occasion, who it's for, the message on the lawn, a team theme if they want one, and any notes.

Set the set-up and take-down times, the price and the deposit. Extras, like extra balloons, add to the balance automatically.

Then tap Save.

Booked, set up, taken down

This is the job page. The three steps across the top show where the job is.

The highlighted card always shows the next thing to do.

Email the customer

First, confirm the booking. Tap Preview confirmation email.

You see the email before it goes out. Change the wording if you like, then send it from your Gmail, or save it as a draft.

Pick the signs

Next, get the crew ready. Pick the worker, check off the signs for this job, and set how many. Each sign shows how many are free on these dates.

For the age, open Numbers and pick from the drop-down. For Maya's tenth birthday, that's the number ten sign. For an age like forty, you'd add a four and a zero.

Add notes, like a gate code.

Preview the text

Tap Preview and text crew. The message fills itself in with the time, the address, the message on the lawn, the team, the signs, your notes, and a link to an example photo.

You can change anything here. When it looks right, tap Text it, and your phone's Messages app opens with everything filled in.

What the crew sees

And here's what Jordan sees on their phone.

Everything the crew needs, in one text.

Add a photo

When the sign is up, add a photo of the finished yard, and tap Mark set up.

The customer gets an email with the photo, so they can see it right away.

Reminders before take-down

The day before pick-up, the app reminds you to email the customer. You can also email yourself a reminder.

Invoice and reviews

After pick-up, tap Mark taken down. The invoice email opens, with the balance due, how to pay, and your review links.

The job then moves to Completed on the Jobs screen.
